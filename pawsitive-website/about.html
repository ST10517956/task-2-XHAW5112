About us

Pawsitive Pet Academy is a small business founded in 2023 by Sarah Smith and based in Durban.

We teach pet care and give hands-on animal training to pet owners, aspiring pet care professionals and animal lovers.

Choose from four six-month Professional Development Programmes or three six-week Practical Skills Courses, and save when you book more than one.
